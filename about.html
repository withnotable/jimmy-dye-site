About Jimmy Dye

A Lifelong Connection to the Lowcountry. Three Decades in Charleston. One Trusted Perspective.

Jimmy Dye is the co-founder of The Cassina Group, a top-ranked boutique real estate firm in Charleston, South Carolina. With more than 20 years of experience, he specializes in luxury homes, waterfront properties, historic estates across Charleston, Sullivan's Island, Isle of Palms, Mount Pleasant, and land throughout the Lowcountry. Jimmy is consistently ranked among the top agents locally and nationally, including RealTrends recognition as a top 1.5% agent in the United States, #8 in South Carolina by sales volume, and a nationally ranked agent.
