Neighborhood GuideMount Pleasant, SC

Old Village

Historic Old Village charms with tree-lined streets, waterfront views, and distinctive architecture along Charleston Harbor's edge.

Historic Waterfront Character

Old Village sits along the banks of Charleston Harbor in Mount Pleasant, where Spanish moss-draped live oaks create natural canopies over quiet residential streets. This historic district preserves the area's maritime heritage with tree-lined avenues that lead to waterfront views and glimpses of Charleston's skyline across the water.

Architectural Heritage

The neighborhood showcases a collection of historic homes and cottages that reflect the area's coastal Southern character. Architecture ranges from traditional Charleston-style houses to historic cottages, many featuring wraparound porches and details that speak to the district's maritime past. The preserved streetscape maintains the intimate scale of this waterfront community.

Waterfront Living

The harbor location provides residents with easy access to waterfront activities and scenic views. The area's connection to Charleston Harbor offers opportunities for boating and water recreation, while tree-shaded streets provide peaceful settings for walking and enjoying the coastal atmosphere.

Local Atmosphere

Old Village maintains a quiet, residential character with its historic district designation helping preserve the area's distinctive coastal charm. The neighborhood's walkable streets and mature landscaping create an intimate community feeling, while its proximity to Charleston Harbor connects it to the broader Lowcountry's maritime culture and traditions.
