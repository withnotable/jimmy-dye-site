Neighborhood GuideMount Pleasant, SC

South of Broad

South of Broad in Mount Pleasant offers coastal living with tree-lined streets, historic charm, and proximity to Charleston's waterways.

Coastal Character and Geography

South of Broad in Mount Pleasant sits on the eastern shores of Charleston Harbor, characterized by its proximity to both the Cooper River and Charleston's historic peninsula. The area features gently rolling terrain with mature live oak canopies and glimpses of tidal waterways that define the Lowcountry landscape.

Architectural Heritage

The neighborhood showcases a mix of architectural styles reflecting the region's coastal heritage. Traditional Charleston-style homes with wide porches and elevated foundations blend with contemporary coastal designs, many featuring elements like dormers, wraparound porches, and hurricane-resistant construction that speaks to generations of coastal living.

Local Culture and Lifestyle

The area maintains a relaxed coastal pace where maritime traditions remain part of daily life. Local culture centers around the water, with many residents enjoying boating, fishing, and waterfront activities. The proximity to Charleston's historic district means easy access to cultural events, festivals, and the renowned culinary scene while maintaining a quieter residential atmosphere.

Natural Spaces and Walkability

Tree-lined streets create natural canopies throughout much of the area, while nearby waterfront access points provide opportunities for kayaking and fishing. The neighborhood's layout encourages walking, with many destinations reachable on foot or by bicycle, connecting residents to both local amenities and the broader Charleston area.
