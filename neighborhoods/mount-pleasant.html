Mount Pleasant

Mount Pleasant blends historic charm with coastal living, featuring tree-lined streets, waterfront views, and a relaxed Lowcountry atmosphere.

Historic Lowcountry Character

Mount Pleasant sits gracefully along Charleston Harbor, embodying the essence of South Carolina's Lowcountry with its moss-draped live oaks, historic plantations, and traditional Southern architecture. The town's roots stretch back to the colonial era, and this heritage remains visible in its preserved antebellum homes and centuries-old churches that dot the landscape.

Waterfront Geography

Positioned between the Cooper and Wando rivers, Mount Pleasant offers abundant water access and coastal views. The town's geography is defined by tidal creeks, marshlands, and several barrier islands, creating a naturally beautiful setting that captures the essence of coastal South Carolina living.

Architectural Heritage

The area showcases a diverse mix of architectural styles, from historic plantation houses and classic Southern cottages to contemporary coastal homes. Many properties feature traditional elements like wraparound porches, tabby foundations, and hurricane shutters that reflect both practical coastal living and regional aesthetic traditions.

Local Culture and Lifestyle

Mount Pleasant maintains a relaxed, coastal pace where boating, fishing, and outdoor activities are woven into daily life. The town hosts various festivals and events throughout the year that celebrate its maritime heritage and Lowcountry culture, while local markets and waterfront dining establishments offer gathering places for the community.
