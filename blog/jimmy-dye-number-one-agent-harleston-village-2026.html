Jimmy Dye Is the #1 Agent in Harleston Village in 2026

Few neighborhoods capture the character of Charleston quite like Harleston Village. With its historic homes, tree-lined streets, Colonial Lake, and proximity to the Battery and King Street, it remains one of the peninsula’s most desirable places to live.

In 2026, Jimmy Dye is the #1 agent in Harleston Village year-to-date, representing some of the neighborhood’s most notable properties, including 110 Beaufain Street and 43 Meeting Street.

Harleston Village has been an important part of Charleston’s residential landscape for centuries. As the city expanded beyond its original settlement, the area became home to grand residences and historic architecture that still define the neighborhood today.

Its streets showcase many of Charleston’s signature architectural styles, from classic Charleston single houses to Federal, Greek Revival, and Victorian homes. The neighborhood’s proximity to the College of Charleston, Colonial Lake, and the waterfront adds to its unique sense of place.

Today, Harleston Village offers something increasingly rare: the opportunity to live surrounded by centuries of history while enjoying the walkability, culture, restaurants, and amenities of downtown Charleston.
