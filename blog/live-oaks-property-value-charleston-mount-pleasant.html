143 Live Oak Drive: The Charm of Old Village Living

There are few places in the Charleston area quite like the Old Village in Mount Pleasant. With its tree-lined streets, historic homes, and unmistakable Lowcountry character, it is a neighborhood that has maintained its identity while continuing to be one of the most desirable places to call home.

Having spent years working in the Charleston real estate market, I have always appreciated what makes the Old Village so special. It is not just the homes themselves, but the setting, the sense of community, and the lifestyle that comes with living here.

My listing at 143 Live Oak Drive is a wonderful opportunity to experience that lifestyle firsthand.

A Home with Classic Lowcountry Appeal

Offering four bedrooms, three-and-a-half bathrooms, and 3,125 square feet, 143 Live Oak combines traditional charm with a flexible layout designed to accommodate a variety of needs.

One of the things I appreciate about this home is its versatility. The first-floor primary suite offers convenience, while the finished room over the garage, complete with its own full bathroom, provides additional flexibility for hosting guests, creating a private home office, or accommodating changing family needs.

The home's formal living and dining rooms provide inviting spaces for gathering, while the overall layout offers the flexibility that today's buyers often look for. It is the kind of home that can adapt to different stages of life without losing the character that makes it special.

Enjoying the Outdoors

In Charleston, some of the best parts of homeownership happen outside. Whether it is enjoying a quiet morning coffee, hosting friends, or spending an afternoon outdoors, having inviting exterior spaces makes a difference.

At 143 Live Oak, the porches, screened-in porch, and patio create several opportunities to enjoy the Lowcountry climate. Mature live oaks and a wooded setting add to the atmosphere, giving the property a natural sense of privacy and the established feel that so many people love about the Old Village.

These are the details that make a house feel connected to its surroundings and to the way people want to live in this part of the world.

Why I Love the Old Village

For me, one of the defining qualities of the Old Village is its sense of place. It has a character that is distinctly Mount Pleasant, with a combination of history, coastal living, and neighborhood charm that is difficult to recreate.

From Shem Creek and its waterfront restaurants to Pitt Street Bridge and the surrounding parks, there is so much to enjoy nearby. The beaches of Sullivan's Island are also just a short drive away, while downtown Charleston remains easily accessible.

Buyers are often drawn to the Charleston area for its lifestyle, and the Old Village is a great example of what makes this market so appealing. You have access to the water, great local restaurants, outdoor recreation, and the conveniences of Mount Pleasant, all while enjoying the character of an established neighborhood.

It is that combination that continues to make the Old Village such a special place to live.

Discover 143 Live Oak Drive

I am excited to represent 143 Live Oak Drive and the opportunity it offers to experience life in one of Mount Pleasant's most beloved neighborhoods.

For buyers looking for classic Lowcountry character, flexible living spaces, and a location that puts so much of the Charleston lifestyle within reach, this home is worth a closer look.
