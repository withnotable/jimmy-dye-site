One Lowcountry, Many Markets: My Experience Across Charleston's Most Desirable Communities

One of the things I enjoy most about working in Charleston real estate is the variety of communities that make up the Lowcountry. From the historic streets of downtown Charleston to the beaches of Sullivan's Island and Isle of Palms, each area has its own personality, lifestyle, and real estate market.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to work with buyers and sellers throughout these communities. While they are all part of the greater Charleston market, understanding what makes each one unique is important.

Sullivan's Island

Sullivan's Island has always held a special place in my life. I've been connected to the island for more than 30 years and lived there for 16 of them. It's a community I know well, both personally and professionally.

The real estate market here is unlike anywhere else in Charleston. From historic cottages to oceanfront homes and larger properties, every property has its own appeal. I'm currently representing two unique Sullivan's Island properties, 2302 Raven Drive and 2871 Ion Avenue, each offering an opportunity to experience what makes this island such a special place to call home.

Isle of Palms

Isle of Palms offers a different kind of coastal lifestyle, with oceanfront homes, waterfront properties, and established residential neighborhoods. Buyers are often drawn to the island for its beach access, boating opportunities, and relaxed atmosphere.

Having experience throughout the coastal communities allows me to help buyers and sellers understand the differences between these markets and what makes each property unique.

Mount Pleasant & Old Village

Mount Pleasant has become one of the most desirable places to live in the Charleston area, offering everything from established neighborhoods to waterfront communities and newer construction.

Within Mount Pleasant, Old Village stands out for its historic homes, tree-lined streets, and waterfront setting. It's a neighborhood with a character all its own, and one where understanding the history and individuality of each property matters. I'm currently representing 143 Live Oak Drive in Old Village, and I always enjoy showcasing the charm and lifestyle that make this neighborhood so special.

Downtown Charleston

Downtown Charleston is one of the most unique real estate markets in the country. Historic architecture, preservation requirements, and the character of each neighborhood all play an important role in determining a property's value.

I've had the opportunity to represent some significant properties on the peninsula, including two sales of 110 Beaufain Street in 2026 and the sale of 43 Meeting Street. I've also been fortunate to build a strong presence in Harleston Village, where I was the number one agent year-to-date in 2026.

Every neighborhood downtown has its own identity, and knowing those differences makes a real difference when working with buyers and sellers.

Johns Island

Johns Island continues to attract buyers looking for more space, waterfront living, and a little more room to enjoy the Lowcountry lifestyle. The market is diverse, with everything from larger homesites and vacant land to waterfront properties and established communities.

I've enjoyed working in this growing market, particularly as more buyers discover what Johns Island has to offer. Understanding the land, location, and opportunities within each community is especially important here.

A Diverse Market, Built on Local Knowledge

Charleston isn't one single real estate market. Sullivan's Island, Isle of Palms, Mount Pleasant, Old Village, downtown Charleston, and Johns Island each offer something different.

Having the opportunity to work across these communities over the years has given me a deep appreciation for what makes each one special. No matter where a client is looking to buy or sell, my goal is always the same: to understand the market, provide honest guidance, and help them make the best possible real estate decision.
