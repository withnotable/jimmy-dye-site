Art on the Beach: Celebrating Art, Community & Life on Sullivan’s Island

There is something special about Sullivan’s Island that goes beyond its beautiful beaches and coastal homes. It is a community with a strong sense of character, a deep appreciation for its history, and a genuine connection to the people and places that make it unique. Events like Art on the Beach are a wonderful reflection of that spirit.

On Sunday, November 8, Sullivan’s Island will welcome the 27th annual Art on the Beach, hosted by Charleston Pro Bono Legal Services. This much-loved event brings together art, food, and some of the island’s most beautiful homes, transforming private residences into pop-up galleries featuring talented local artists and makers. Guests can explore the island, enjoy culinary offerings, and experience the creativity that makes the Lowcountry such a special place.

Having called Sullivan’s Island home for 16 years and spent more than three decades connected to the island, I have always appreciated what makes this community so distinctive. It is a place where people take pride in supporting local businesses, celebrating local talent, and giving back. Art on the Beach brings all of those things together while supporting an important cause. Proceeds from the event benefit Charleston Pro Bono Legal Services, which provides free civil legal assistance to individuals and families throughout our community.

Stop by Two of My Sullivan’s Island Listings

At The Cassina Group, we are excited to be part of the Art on the Beach weekend. I will be opening the doors to two of my Sullivan’s Island listings during the festivities. If you are spending the day exploring the island, I invite you to stop by and take a look.

2871 Ion Avenue

Set on an elevated corner lot, 2871 Ion Avenue is an impressive coastal residence that offers a wonderful combination of space, thoughtful design, and island living. With more than 5,200 square feet, four bedrooms, and four-and-a-half bathrooms, the home offers a scale and flexibility that is increasingly difficult to find on Sullivan’s Island.

The interior features expansive gathering spaces, a gourmet kitchen with oversized islands, beautiful wood floors, and thoughtfully designed living areas. Two spacious primary suites, a private office, and an upstairs loft provide flexibility for both everyday living and hosting family and friends.

Outside, the home continues to impress with a private swimming pool, inviting porches, and elevated views. It is a property that offers plenty of room to enjoy everything that makes island living so appealing.

2302 Raven Drive

For those drawn to exceptional outdoor living and marshfront views, 2302 Raven Drive is a property worth experiencing in person. Situated on approximately three-quarters of an acre, this elevated coastal residence offers more than 6,300 square feet, five bedrooms, five full bathrooms, and two half bathrooms.

The home combines timeless architecture with impressive scale and detail. Soaring ceilings, extensive custom millwork, and four fireplaces create a warm and inviting interior, while multiple gathering spaces offer plenty of room for entertaining. The home also features an elevator, private office, wet bar, and spacious primary suite.

One of the property's most appealing features is its connection to the outdoors. With a private swimming pool, screened porch, generous outdoor living areas, and beautiful marshfront surroundings, 2302 Raven offers a setting that captures the relaxed coastal lifestyle Sullivan's Island is known for. Its location also puts you just moments from the island's restaurants, shops, and beach.

Come Explore Sullivan’s Island

Whether you are attending Art on the Beach, supporting local artists, or simply enjoying an afternoon on the island, I hope you will make time to visit.

Both 2871 Ion Avenue and 2302 Raven Drive will be open during the event hours, giving visitors an opportunity to experience two very different homes and get a closer look at what makes Sullivan's Island such a special place to call home.

Art on the Beach is a great reminder that what makes this island so desirable is about more than the homes and the beach. It is the people, the creativity, and the sense of community that bring it all together.

I hope to see you out there!
