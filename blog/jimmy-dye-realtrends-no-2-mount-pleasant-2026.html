Jimmy Dye Named No. 2 Real Estate Agent in Mount Pleasant by RealTrends

The Cassina Group Co-Founder has been recognized among South Carolina's top-producing real estate professionals in the 2026 RealTrends Verified rankings.

The Cassina Group is proud to announce that Co-Founder and Realtor Jimmy Dye has been recognized among the leading real estate professionals in the country in the 2026 RealTrends Verified rankings.

Based on 2025 sales data, Dye ranked No. 2 in Mount Pleasant and No. 28 in South Carolina by individual sales volume. He was also recognized as a RealTrends Verified Top Agent by Volume, an achievement that places him among a distinguished group of real estate professionals in the United States whose production has been independently verified by RealTrends.
